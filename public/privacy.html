Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 29, 2026

Welcome to SoulThread. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application (the "App"). Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this Privacy Policy, please do not access the App.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information about you in a variety of ways. The information we may collect via the App includes:

  • Personal Data: Personally identifiable information, such as your name, email address, and profile picture, that you voluntarily give to us when you choose to participate in various activities related to the App, such as account registration.
  • Health & Care Data: Information you provide within the NEHA assistant, including patient profiles, medicine logs, uploaded medical reports, and caretaker community posts.
  • Device Data: Information our servers automatically collect when you access the App, such as your native actions that are integral to the App, as well as device information like operating system, device ID, and IP address.

2. Use of Your Information

Having accurate information about you permits us to provide you with a smooth, efficient, and customized experience. Specifically, we may use information collected about you via the App to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Provide, operate, and maintain our App.
  • Improve, personalize, and expand our App.
  • Understand and analyze how you use our App.
  • Send you emails or push notifications regarding your account or the App.

3. Disclosure of Your Information

We do not share your personal information with third parties except as described in this Privacy Policy, such as complying with the law or protecting our rights. We do not sell your personal or health data to third parties, and we do not use your data for advertising purposes.

4. Security & Storage of Your Information

We use administrative, technical, and physical security measures to help protect your personal information. All health data, reports, and personal information are securely stored using Google Firebase. Data is encrypted both in transit and at rest, and is stored in secure data centers (India/Singapore region). While we have taken reasonable steps to secure the personal information you provide to us, please be aware that despite our efforts, no security measures are perfect or impenetrable.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your personal data only for as long as is necessary for the purposes set out in this Privacy Policy, such as maintaining your active account and providing our services. When you request the deletion of your account and associated data, we will delete or anonymize your information within 30 days. You can request deletion directly within the App settings or by contacting us at support@soulthread.in.
